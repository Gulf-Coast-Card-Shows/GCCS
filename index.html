Frequently Asked Questions

How often is this card show calendar updated?

New shows and corrections go up weekly, usually as soon as an organizer submits details or a collector flags an error. Check the "Last updated" note in the footer for the most recent refresh.

Is it free to attend Gulf Coast card shows?

It depends on the show — many are free to walk in, while others charge a small entry fee (usually $0–$10). Each show card lists its entry fee, and free shows are marked clearly so you can filter for them.

What kinds of cards will I find at these shows?

Most shows feature a mix of sports cards (baseball, football, basketball) and Pokémon TCG, with several also carrying Magic: The Gathering, Yu-Gi-Oh!, One Piece, Disney Lorcana, and general collectibles. Use the "Filter by Card Type" control to narrow the list to what you collect.

Which states and cities do you cover?

The calendar currently tracks shows across Florida, Alabama, and Mississippi along the Gulf Coast — including Pensacola, Mobile, Biloxi, Tallahassee, Saraland, and Huntsville. Use the "Browse by State" links to jump straight to a state's listings.

How do I get my card show listed?

Click "Add Your Show" anywhere on the site and fill in the event details — organizer, date, location, entry fee, and a website or social link. Submissions are reviewed and typically posted within a few days.
